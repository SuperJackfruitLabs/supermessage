Fleet card directions

Three ways to redesign the Lock Screen Live Activity, each shown in the three moments that matter: an agent working, an agent that needs you, and a turn that finished. Pick one, or mix parts.

Card width 344pt at the iPhone 13 mini's Lock Screen · max height 160pt · supermessage dark tokens · avatars are placeholders for the Guild avatars

A + C

The track and the dashboard, together

The chosen mix. C's counts sit on top, with a fixed time instead of a ticking one. The most important agent gets A's headline, clock and track; the agent that needs you comes first, then the one that was busy most recently. Everyone else gets one of C's compact rows, and rows that don't fit drop into the count. The dashed line is the 160pt limit.

What each needs beyond today's card

  • All three: the hub sends the turn's phase (thinking, tools, writing) and each agent's avatar key. The app caches the Guild avatars into the App Group when it runs, because the card can't load images from the network. The header shows a fixed time instead of a ticking one.
  • A, the track: the richest moment and the most familiar pattern. It shows one agent at a time, so a second busy agent is a single line.
  • B, headline and avatars: the most personal. It suits the Guild, where agents have faces and names. A result line on Done needs the hub to send the answer's first sentence, which goes to Apple in plaintext like the rest of the card.
  • C, compact dashboard: the best at three or more agents, and the plainest at one. It changes least from today's card.
  • The decision looks the same in every direction: the only amber element, with two big buttons. It always takes the top of the card.
